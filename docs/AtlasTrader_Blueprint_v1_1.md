# AtlasTrader Blueprint v1.0

## Vision

AtlasTrader is a macro-first trading platform that explains every
decision.

## Core Philosophy

-   Macro before technicals
-   Every trade has a thesis
-   Every trade has an invalidation
-   Every decision must be explainable

## Modules

-   Macro Engine
-   Currency Strength Matrix
-   Sentiment Engine
-   Technical Engine
-   Voting/Confidence Engine
-   Journal Engine
-   Analytics Engine

## Journal

Capture: - Pair, direction, entry, SL, TP - Confidence score - Profit
and Loss - Macro, technical and sentiment factors - Loss cause
classification - Trade quality grade - Outcome grade - Post-trade
reflection

## Future Ideas

-   Devil's Advocate module
-   Daily/weekly reports
-   Explainable AI

## Constraints & Status

-   Development environment: Android phone only (no PC)
-   Backtesting: on hold, not planned for at least 1 year
